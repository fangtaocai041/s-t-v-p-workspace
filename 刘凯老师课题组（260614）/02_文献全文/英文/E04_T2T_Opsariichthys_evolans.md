# Telomere-to-telomere gap-free genome assembly of Opsariichthys evolans

**期刊**: Scientific Data, 2026  
**DOI**: 10.1038/s41597-026-06588-7  
**PMCID**: PMC12917274  
**作者**: Wang P, Wang X, Yin D, Liu J, Jiang M, Liu K.

## 核心
- T2T gap-free genome: 886.9 Mb, 39条染色体
- Contig N50: 25.44 Mb, BUSCO 99.34%
- 支持Opsariichthys与Zacco属的分类修订
