# Evaluating Fishing Ban Effectiveness Through Spatiotemporal Changes in Genetic Diversity of Parabramis pekinensis

**期刊**: Ecology and Evolution, 2026  
**DOI**: 10.1002/ece3.72775  
**PMCID**: PMC13054008  
**作者**: Wang Z, Yin D, Liu J, Wang P, Liu S, Huang Z, Li W, Cao M, Liu K.

## 摘要
To scientifically evaluate spatiotemporal changes in genetic diversity of the bream (Parabramis pekinensis) population in the lower Yangtze River following the Yangtze fishing ban, temporal genetic diversity trends (2022-2024) were analyzed using mitochondrial Cytochrome b (Cytb) from three geographical groups (Anqing, Dangtu, Changshu), while genetic differentiation and gene flow patterns among seven geographical groups in 2024 were assessed through whole-genome resequencing. The analysis exhibited a fluctuating upward trend interannually in genetic diversity.

## 核心发现
- 单倍型多样性(Hd): 0.918→0.957 (2022→2024)
- 共享单倍型: 8→12
- 遗传分化Fst: 0.00775→0 (表明基因流增强)
- 2022年极端低水位导致2023年核苷酸多样性下降
- 7个地理群体间无显著遗传分化，Fst范围0-0.0032
